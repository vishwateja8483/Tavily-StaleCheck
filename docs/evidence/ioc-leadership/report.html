Limitations

  1. All relations, observations, rationales and proposed edits are model-generated, not independently verified semantic entailment.
  2. Verbatim quotation is checked against retained excerpts only. Domain preference does not authenticate a primary source; distinct URLs do not prove independence.
  3. Current-source disagreement does not establish that an answer was once correct. Temporal change is not established by this pipeline.
  4. Publication/update dates are provider estimates, not effective dates. Retrieval time and recent publication do not establish factual currency.
  5. Evidence strength and freshness are uncalibrated metadata heuristics, not probabilities of correctness or currency: freshness only reflects how recent the newest quoted source is. Proposed edits are never automatically applied.
  6. The journey deadline is cooperative. Socket timeouts do not impose a hard bound on DNS, response headers or a detached response socket.
  7. Usage is a known subtotal plus completeness, not a monetary spending guarantee. Unknown routing means fallback behavior is unknown.
  8. Unsigned transcripts and approval records establish internal consistency, not authenticated execution, consent, source truth or live provider compatibility.
